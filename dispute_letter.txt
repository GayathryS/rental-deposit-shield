APPLICABLE LAW — California Civil Code § 1950.5
  A landlord may deduct only for (1) unpaid rent, (2) cleaning to restore the
  unit to its move-in level of cleanliness, (3) repair of damage beyond normal

  permits. Normal wear and tear may NOT be deducted, and the landlord must
  provide an itemized statement within 21 days of move-out.
